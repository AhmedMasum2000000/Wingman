Reset M1D1?

This clears the audio, the analysis, the chords and the MIDI. Nothing else in your project changes.

MIDI out

M1D1 sends its three parts as live MIDI, so any instrument can play them: a synth, a piano, a sampler, hardware.

Chordschannel 1Basschannel 2Leadchannel 3

Send these parts
In FL Studio (the plugin)
  1. Open M1D1's wrapper settings (the gear) and set MIDI → Output port to a number, for example 7.
  2. On each instrument you want to play, set MIDI → Input port to the same number.
  3. Give each instrument the channel of its part (1, 2 or 3), press Play on a part in M1D1, and the instrument plays it.
  4. An instrument on that port plays every part ticked above. To play one part on it, untick the others. Then press Play in FL Studio.
